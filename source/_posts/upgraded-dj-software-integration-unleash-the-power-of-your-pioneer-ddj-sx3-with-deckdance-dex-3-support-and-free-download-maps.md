**The 4-Channel Pioneer DDJ-SX3 DJ controller is now DEX 3 and DEX 3 RE supported (Download the map below).**

Expanding on the features of the DDJ-SX2 controller, a highly-popular DJ controller with [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) customers, the DDJ-SX3 is ideal for professional mobile DJs performing at club nights, parties, weddings and corporate events. A perfect pairing with DEX 3 whether you mix music, music videos, or host karaoke.

The [DDJ-SX3](https://www.pioneerdj.com/en-us/product/controller/ddj-sx3/black/overview/) ($1099.00 on most DJ Equipment websites) improves upon the previous DDJ-SX3 design with a familiar layout and lower latency jog wheels. A nice ‘get’ for Karaoke Hosts using DEX 3 — the DDJ-SX3 also now features 3 microphone inputs, each with independent EQs and FX. DJs can also connect 2 computers at the same time for easy DJ changeovers (or instant backup) via the dual USB ports.

Pioneer DJ packed the DJ controller with a 4-channel audio interface that more than rivals the equipment found in your average professional recording studio. Pioneer states the new built in pro audio interface is an industry best. It should sound great even at ultra low latency.

* Channels: 4
* Frequency response: 20Hz – 20kHz

**Connectivity**

* Phono inputs: 2 (RCA)
* CD/Line inputs: 2 (RCA)
* Mic inputs 3
* USB ports: 2

* Master out: 2 (RCA, XLR)

* Mixer: 4-channel
